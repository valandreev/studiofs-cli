# 09. Check Your Infrastructure

Before installing StudioFS, make sure the following are available from your workstation:

- [ ] **Tailscale** is connected, and the server is reachable on the private network.
- [ ] **PostgreSQL** is reachable, and the required credentials and permissions are ready.
- [ ] **S3** is reachable at the correct endpoint and bucket, and the keys have the required permissions.

When all three checks are complete, continue with the installation chapter for your system: [Windows](10-install-studiofs-on-windows.md), [macOS](11-install-studiofs-on-macos.md), or [Linux](12-install-studiofs-on-linux.md).
