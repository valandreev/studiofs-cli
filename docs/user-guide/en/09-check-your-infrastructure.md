# 09. Check your infrastructure
> **Work in progress**

Before installing StudioFS, check that all required components are reachable from your workstation:

- [ ] **Tailscale** is connected, and the server is reachable on the private network.
- [ ] **PostgreSQL** is reachable; the credentials and required permissions are ready.
- [ ] **S3** is reachable at the correct endpoint and bucket; the keys have the required permissions.

If you have checked all the boxes, proceed to the installation guide for your system: [Windows](10-install-studiofs-on-windows.md), [macOS](11-install-studiofs-on-macos.md), or [Linux](12-install-studiofs-on-linux.md).
