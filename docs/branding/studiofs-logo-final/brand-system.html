Wordmark study

Space Grotesk

The best fit for the icon. It has enough geometry to feel engineered, but still has personality, so the mark does not become too cold or too enterprise-generic.

Recommended primary wordmark

IBM Plex Sans

The strongest “infrastructure / CLI / serious tooling” direction. Very credible for docs and product UI, but the wordmark feels a touch more institutional than distinctive.

Manrope

The softest and most polished option. Good if you want StudioFS to feel more productized and approachable, but it loses a bit of the filesystem toughness that the icon already has.
